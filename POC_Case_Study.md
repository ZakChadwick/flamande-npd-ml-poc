# Demand Forecasting POC:  Wholesale Sauce Distribution

## The Challenge
Wholesale sauce distributors face 15-20% inventory waste from spoilage and stockouts, 
directly impacting already-thin profit margins (3-5%).

## Our Approach
Machine learning model combining:
- Historical sales patterns
- Seasonal trends (BBQ season, holidays)
- External factors (weather, promotions)
- Shelf-life constraints

## Results
**Baseline vs. ML-Optimized (90-day simulation)**

| Metric | Baseline | With ML | Improvement |
|--------|----------|---------|-------------|
| Inventory Turnover | 6.2x | 8.4x | +35% |
| Stockout Rate | 12% | 4% | -67% |
| Waste from Spoilage | £3,800 | £1,200 | -68% |
| Holding Costs | £12,500 | £8,750 | -30% |

## Business Impact
- **£26,000 annual savings** for mid-sized distributor (10 SKUs)
- **2. 1 percentage point** margin improvement
- **ROI achieved in 3 months**

## Next Steps
Scale to full product catalog (50+ SKUs), integrate with existing ERP system.
